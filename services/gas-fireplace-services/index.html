Gas Fireplace Services in San Francisco, CA

Gas fireplaces are the most popular heating appliance choice in San Francisco, but they require regular professional service to operate safely and efficiently.

Call +1 925-433-7889 — Available 24/7

Gas Fireplace Cleaning & Tune-Up

Annual gas fireplace service by AL Chimney Service includes:

  • Complete cleaning of glass doors and fireplace interior
  • Burner cleaning and inspection
  • Artificial log and ember media cleaning and repositioning
  • Inspection and testing of all gas components (valve, pilot, thermocouple/thermopile, igniter)
  • Flue and vent inspection for blockages and proper draft
  • Carbon monoxide and gas leak check

Gas Fireplace Repairs

Pilot & Ignition Problems

Gas fireplaces that won't light or won't stay lit typically have thermocouple or thermopile failures. We test and replace these components on the spot in most cases.

Gas Valve Replacement

A failing gas valve must be replaced by a qualified technician. We carry common replacement valves for major gas fireplace brands.

Remote Control & Receiver Replacement

Modern gas fireplaces use radio-frequency or Wi-Fi controls. We repair and replace control systems for all major fireplace manufacturers.

Glass Seal & Door Replacement

Cracked or fogged glass doors reduce safety and appearance. We replace glass panels and gaskets for all fireplace models.
