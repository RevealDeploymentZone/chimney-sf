Types of Chimney Inspections We Perform

AL Chimney Service San Francisco performs all three levels of chimney inspection in accordance with NFPA 211 standards — the same standards followed by certified chimney professionals across the United States.

Level 1 Chimney Inspection

A Level 1 inspection covers all accessible portions of the chimney exterior and interior, including the firebox, smoke chamber, damper, and visible flue sections. This is the standard annual inspection recommended for chimneys that have been in continuous service with the same appliance and no significant changes. It verifies that the chimney is structurally sound, free of obstructions, and free of combustible deposits.

Level 2 Chimney Inspection

A Level 2 inspection includes everything in a Level 1, plus a video scan of the entire flue interior using a specialized camera. This level is required when:

  • Buying or selling a home in San Francisco
  • Changing the fuel type or appliance connected to the chimney
  • After a chimney fire or natural disaster
  • After any event that may have caused structural damage

We provide full video footage and a written report documenting all findings — valuable documentation for real estate transactions and insurance claims.

Level 3 Chimney Inspection

A Level 3 inspection is performed when a serious structural defect is suspected. It may involve removal of components such as the chimney crown, interior walls, or other elements to access and fully evaluate the condition of the chimney. This level is reserved for situations where Level 1 or 2 inspection reveals conditions that cannot be fully assessed otherwise.

What We Check During an Inspection

  • Firebox — condition of firebrick and refractory panels
  • Damper — operation, seating, and corrosion
  • Smoke shelf and smoke chamber
  • Flue lining — cracks, spalling, mortar joint deterioration
  • Chimney crown — cracks and water infiltration
  • Chimney cap — presence, fit, and condition
  • Exterior masonry — spalling, efflorescence, mortar erosion
  • Flashing — seal at roof-chimney junction
  • Creosote buildup level and classification
