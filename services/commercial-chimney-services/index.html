Commercial Chimney Needs Are Different from Residential

Running commercial chimney service for a San Francisco business or multi-unit property isn't just a bigger version of residential chimney work. The stakes are different, the regulatory environment is different, and the consequences of deferring maintenance are much more serious than they are for a single-family home.

For a business, a chimney or exhaust system failure can mean a forced closure by the fire department or health department, a failed insurance inspection, or liability exposure if a tenant or guest is injured. For a property manager overseeing a multi-family building, deferred chimney maintenance across dozens of units can quickly become a compliance nightmare and a genuine fire safety issue affecting many people.

AL Chimney Service works with commercial clients throughout San Francisco to develop service programs that meet their specific operational needs — minimizing disruption to tenants and business operations while ensuring full compliance with all applicable codes and regulations. We understand that for a restaurant or hotel, scheduling is everything. We work around your operating hours and can perform service during off-peak times or overnight when necessary.

Restaurant and Kitchen Hood Exhaust Systems

San Francisco has one of the most concentrated restaurant scenes in the country, and a significant number of those restaurants use wood-burning equipment — wood-fired pizza ovens, open-hearth grills, and wood-burning rotisseries. These applications produce dramatically higher levels of grease-laden vapor and creosote than residential wood burning, and they demand much more frequent cleaning.

Under NFPA 96 (Standard for Ventilation Control and Fire Protection of Commercial Cooking Operations) and the California Fire Code, commercial kitchen exhaust systems require inspection and cleaning at intervals based on cooking volume and the type of food being cooked. For a high-volume wood-fired oven, that can mean quarterly or even monthly cleaning — not annual.

What we provide for San Francisco restaurants and commercial kitchens:

  • Scheduled cleaning of the chimney flue, smoke chamber, and smoke shelf serving wood-burning cooking appliances
  • Grease trap and grease collection container service for wood-fired systems
  • Inspection of the appliance-to-flue connection and all accessible ductwork for grease accumulation
  • Cleaning frequency recommendations based on your volume and menu — we document our findings so you have a defensible basis for your cleaning schedule
  • Written service reports for health department, fire marshal, and insurance carrier compliance
  • Emergency service availability for restaurants that need a cleaning before an inspection or after an unexpected grease buildup

Grease in a chimney is a fire hazard of an entirely different magnitude than creosote. A grease fire in a poorly maintained restaurant chimney can reach temperatures exceeding 2,000°F and can destroy a commercial kitchen — and the surrounding building — very quickly. This is not an area where it pays to cut corners or defer service.

Multi-Family Building Chimney Maintenance

San Francisco's multi-family residential building stock ranges from Victorian-era Edwardians with ornamental fireplaces in every unit, to mid-century apartment towers with centralized heating systems, to modern condo buildings with decorative gas fireplaces in individual units. We have experience with all of these scenarios and the unique challenges each one presents.

For property managers and HOAs overseeing buildings with multiple fireplaces or heating appliances, coordinated annual service is both a safety requirement and a practical operational necessity. Here's how we approach building-wide service programs:

  1. Initial building assessment: We survey the building to document all chimney systems, appliance types, flue configurations, and current condition. This gives us and the property manager a clear picture of the scope of work and any priority items.
  2. Service scheduling: We work with property management to schedule unit-by-unit service in the most efficient sequence, minimizing the number of separate appointments and resident disruptions.
  3. Standardized reporting: Every unit serviced receives a written inspection and service report. Building management receives a consolidated summary report covering all units.
  4. Priority issue flagging: Any unit with a safety deficiency — cracked liner, missing cap, improper clearances — is flagged as a priority and the property manager is notified immediately with photos and documentation.
  5. Annual contract scheduling: We establish a recurring annual service contract so building management doesn't have to remember to schedule — we contact you when it's time.

For buildings with many units, we can typically service multiple apartments per day and complete large buildings within a week, working around tenant schedules as needed.

Compliance, Inspection Reports, and Documentation

Commercial chimney compliance in San Francisco involves several overlapping regulatory frameworks. Property managers and business owners need to satisfy requirements from the San Francisco Fire Department, the California State Fire Marshal, the Bay Area Air Quality Management District, and their insurance carriers — sometimes all at once.

AL Chimney Service provides the documentation commercial clients need:

  • NFPA 211-compliant inspection reports: Our written inspection reports document the condition of every component in the chimney system and classify any deficiencies according to Level 1, 2, or 3 inspection standards.
  • Service certificates: Dated, signed certificates of cleaning and inspection for fire marshal and insurance carrier requirements.
  • Before-and-after photo documentation: We photograph significant findings before cleaning and document the condition after service — essential for demonstrating due diligence to regulators and insurers.
  • BAAQMD compliance documentation: For properties with wood-burning appliances, we provide documentation that appliances are BAAQMD-compliant and advise on Spare the Air compliance procedures for tenants.
  • Repair estimates and priority rankings: Any deficiencies found are documented with repair recommendations, cost estimates, and priority rankings so property management can make informed decisions.

We understand that for a commercial property manager, documentation isn't optional — it's the difference between passing an inspection and failing one. We take documentation as seriously as the technical work itself.

Service Contracts for Commercial Properties

One of the most common challenges we hear from commercial property managers in San Francisco is the difficulty of tracking maintenance schedules across multiple systems and locations. When you're managing several buildings with dozens of chimneys and fireplaces, it's easy for annual service to slip — especially when you're dealing with the hundred other things that demand attention in a commercial property.

Our commercial service contracts solve this problem by taking the scheduling burden off your desk entirely. Here's what a typical service contract includes:

  • Automatic scheduling contact from us each year before the service is due — you don't have to remember to call
  • Priority scheduling for contract clients during the busy fall season
  • Locked-in service rates for the duration of the contract — no price surprises
  • Priority response for emergency calls — contract clients go to the front of the line for urgent situations
  • Consolidated invoicing — one invoice per building rather than separate invoices per unit
  • Annual documentation package — all service reports and certificates compiled and delivered to property management at the end of each service cycle

Service contracts are available on annual or multi-year terms. Contact us to discuss the right structure for your portfolio.
