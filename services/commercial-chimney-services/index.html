Commercial Chimney Services We Provide

Restaurant & Commercial Kitchen Chimney Service

Restaurants with wood-burning ovens, pizza ovens, and open-hearth cooking require frequent chimney cleaning due to high grease and creosote accumulation. We provide scheduled commercial kitchen chimney cleaning programs to maintain code compliance and prevent fire hazards.

Apartment & Condominium Building Services

San Francisco's large multi-unit residential buildings often have multiple fireplaces and heating appliances requiring coordinated annual service. We work with property managers and HOAs to schedule efficient building-wide service programs with minimal disruption to residents.

Hotel & Hospitality Fireplace Service

Hotels and bed-and-breakfast properties with decorative and functional fireplaces in guest rooms and common areas rely on us for inspections, cleaning, and repairs that meet all California hospitality and fire safety requirements.

Commercial Dryer Vent Cleaning

Laundromats, hotels, and apartment buildings with shared laundry facilities require more frequent dryer vent cleaning due to high usage volume. See our Dryer Vent Cleaning page for details.

Compliance & Documentation

We provide written inspection reports, service certificates, and documentation for property managers, insurance carriers, and San Francisco Fire Department compliance requirements.
