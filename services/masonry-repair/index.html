Chimney Masonry Repair Services

Tuckpointing (Mortar Joint Repointing)

Tuckpointing is the process of removing deteriorated mortar from brick joints to a depth of approximately 3/4 inch and replacing it with fresh mortar. We carefully match the mortar color, texture, and composition to the existing joints for a seamless appearance — especially important for San Francisco's many historic Victorian and Edwardian homes.

Spalling Brick Repair

Spalling — where the face of a brick breaks off or flakes — is caused by water infiltration and freeze-thaw cycling. We replace individual spalled bricks or entire courses using salvaged period-appropriate bricks when available.

Efflorescence Treatment

White staining (efflorescence) on chimney bricks is caused by water carrying soluble salts to the surface. We treat efflorescence at its source by correcting water infiltration and cleaning the masonry surface.

Chimney Rebuild

Sections of chimney that have experienced severe seismic damage, freeze-thaw deterioration, or water damage may require partial or full rebuild. See our Chimney Rebuilding service page.

Historic Preservation Experience

Many San Francisco chimneys are part of historic structures. AL Chimney Service works with original lime-based mortars and period-appropriate materials to maintain the historic integrity of Victorian, Edwardian, and Craftsman-era homes throughout San Francisco's neighborhoods.
