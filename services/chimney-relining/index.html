Chimney Relining Services in San Francisco, CA

A cracked or deteriorated chimney liner is one of the most dangerous conditions a San Francisco homeowner can face. Relining your chimney with a new stainless steel or cast-in-place liner restores safety and maximizes heating efficiency.

Why Chimney Relining Is Critical

The chimney liner serves three essential functions: it contains combustion gases and sparks, protects the surrounding masonry from heat, and provides the correct size flue for efficient draft. When a liner is cracked, corroded, or missing, combustion gases including deadly carbon monoxide can enter your San Francisco home's living spaces.

California building codes and insurance requirements typically mandate that a functioning liner be in place for all fireplace and heating appliance installations.

Types of Chimney Liners We Install

Stainless Steel Flexible Liner

The most commonly installed liner type, flexible stainless steel liners can navigate offsets and bends in the flue. They are available in single-wall and insulated double-wall configurations for different fuel types and appliance requirements.

Stainless Steel Rigid Liner

Rigid liners offer the smoothest interior surface for maximum draft performance. Used in straight chimneys, they are ideal for high-efficiency wood stoves and gas appliances.

Cast-in-Place Liner

A pumped, cast-in-place liner creates a seamless, insulated flue inside the existing masonry. Ideal for chimneys with irregular flue shapes or severely deteriorated interiors.

When Is Relining Required?

  • Cracked, spalled, or missing clay tile liner sections
  • Switching from one fuel type to another (wood to gas, etc.)
  • Installing a new high-efficiency appliance that requires a different flue size
  • After a chimney fire that damaged the existing liner
  • Older San Francisco homes where the original liner has deteriorated
