Pellet Stove Services in San Francisco, CA

Pellet stoves are efficient, BAAQMD-compliant heating appliances that require specialized annual cleaning and maintenance. AL Chimney Service San Francisco services all pellet stove brands.

Call +1 925-433-7889 — Available 24/7

Why Pellet Stoves Need Professional Service

Pellet stoves are more mechanically complex than wood stoves or fireplaces, incorporating auger motors, combustion fans, heat exchangers, and electronic controls. Annual professional service ensures reliable operation and prevents costly breakdowns.

Our Pellet Stove Cleaning Service

  • Full disassembly and cleaning of combustion chamber and ash pan
  • Heat exchanger tube cleaning for maximum efficiency
  • Auger tube and hopper cleaning
  • Combustion and convection fan cleaning and inspection
  • Exhaust vent pipe cleaning and inspection
  • Igniter inspection and testing
  • Control board inspection
  • Door gasket and glass cleaning

Pellet Stove Repairs

We repair all pellet stove brands and models, including:

  • Auger motor replacement
  • Combustion and convection fan motor replacement
  • Igniter replacement
  • Control board diagnosis and replacement
  • Snap disc and thermal switch replacement
  • Combustion door gasket replacement
