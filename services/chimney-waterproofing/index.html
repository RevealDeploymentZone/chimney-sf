Chimney Waterproofing in San Francisco, CA

San Francisco's rainy winters and coastal humidity make chimney waterproofing one of the most valuable investments a homeowner can make to protect their chimney system long-term.

Call +1 925-433-7889 — Available 24/7

Why Waterproof Your Chimney in San Francisco?

Brick and mortar are highly porous materials that readily absorb rainwater and moisture. In San Francisco's climate, this leads to:

  • Freeze-thaw spalling (even in mild SF winters, nighttime temperatures cause moisture expansion)
  • Accelerated mortar erosion
  • Efflorescence (white mineral staining on brick faces)
  • Internal water damage to flue liner and firebox
  • Mold and mildew in the chimney structure

Our Waterproofing Products

We use only vapor-permeable (breathable) waterproof sealants that allow moisture already inside the masonry to escape, while preventing new water from entering. Impermeable sealants trap moisture and accelerate damage — we never use paint or non-breathable coatings on chimney masonry.

Our professional-grade chimney waterproofing products are specifically formulated for masonry surfaces and provide 10+ years of protection in typical conditions.

The Waterproofing Process

  1. Full chimney inspection to identify existing damage needing repair before sealing
  2. Cleaning of all masonry surfaces
  3. Repair of any cracks, eroded mortar, or damaged areas
  4. Application of breathable waterproof sealant in two coats
  5. Inspection and documentation
