Chimney & Fireplace FAQ — San Francisco

Answers to the most common chimney and fireplace questions from San Francisco homeowners. Can't find your answer? Call us at +1 925-433-7889 — available 24/7.

How often should I have my chimney swept in San Francisco?

The National Fire Protection Association (NFPA 211) recommends that chimneys be inspected at least annually and cleaned as needed. In San Francisco, where coastal humidity and fog accelerate creosote buildup and moisture damage, annual sweeping is especially important.

  • Wood-burning fireplaces: After every 1–2 cords burned, or annually
  • Gas fireplaces: Annually, even without wood burning
  • Newly purchased homes: Before first use, regardless of prior history

What does a chimney inspection include?

AL Chimney Service performs all three NFPA 211 inspection levels:

  • Level 1: Visual inspection of all accessible portions — firebox, damper, flue, crown, cap, exterior masonry
  • Level 2: Everything in Level 1, plus video scanning of the complete flue interior. Required for home sales and after chimney events.
  • Level 3: For suspected serious structural defects. May involve partial disassembly for access.

All inspections include a written report. Learn more: Chimney Inspection Services

How do I know if my chimney needs repair?

Common warning signs that your San Francisco chimney needs repair:

  • White staining (efflorescence) on the exterior brick — indicates water infiltration
  • Crumbling or missing mortar joints between bricks
  • Visible cracks in the chimney crown (top slab)
  • Water stains inside the firebox after rain
  • Strong odors from the fireplace when not in use
  • Smoke entering the room during fireplace use
  • Visible cracks in clay tile flue liner sections

If you see any of these, contact us immediately. Learn more: Chimney Repair Services

What is chimney relining and when is it needed?

Chimney relining installs a new liner (stainless steel or cast-in-place) inside the existing flue to safely contain combustion gases and protect the surrounding masonry. Relining is needed when:

  • The existing clay tile liner is cracked, spalled, or missing sections
  • Switching from one fuel type to another (e.g., wood to gas)
  • Installing a new high-efficiency appliance requiring a different flue size
  • After a chimney fire that damaged the liner

Learn more: Chimney Relining Services

Do I need a chimney inspection when buying a San Francisco home?

Yes, strongly recommended. A Level 2 chimney inspection is the standard required when purchasing a home, as it includes video scanning of the entire flue interior and a written report. Many mortgage lenders and home insurance companies require chimney inspection documentation. A pre-purchase chimney inspection can reveal thousands of dollars in needed repairs before you close the deal.

Can you remove animals or birds nesting in my chimney?

Yes. Common chimney wildlife in San Francisco includes birds (chimney swifts, starlings, pigeons), raccoons, squirrels, and bats. We provide humane removal appropriate to the species and California wildlife protection laws, followed by complete chimney cleaning and chimney cap installation to prevent future entry.

Note: Chimney swifts are federally protected migratory birds and cannot be removed during active nesting season (typically April–October). We can advise on your specific situation.

Do you offer dryer vent cleaning in San Francisco?

Yes. Clogged dryer vents are a leading cause of residential fires. The U.S. Fire Administration reports approximately 2,900 dryer fires annually, with lint buildup as the leading cause. We recommend professional dryer vent cleaning at least once per year, or more frequently if you dry heavy items regularly.

What are San Francisco's wood-burning restrictions?

The Bay Area Air Quality Management District (BAAQMD) issues Spare the Air alerts — typically during winter months — during which wood burning in fireplaces and stoves is prohibited throughout the Bay Area, including San Francisco. Burning during a Spare the Air day can result in fines.

Additionally, only EPA-certified, low-emission wood stoves may be newly installed in the Bay Area. Gas fireplaces and electric fireplaces are not subject to Spare the Air restrictions. We can advise you on compliant appliance options.

What causes chimney odors in summer?

Chimney odors in summer, especially in hot weather, are almost always caused by creosote and soot deposits that release odor compounds when heated by warm air. The negative air pressure inside your San Francisco home during summer pulls warm, odor-laden air down the chimney into your living spaces. Annual chimney sweeping eliminates the deposits causing the odor. A top-sealing damper also helps by sealing the flue when not in use.

Is it safe to use my fireplace after an earthquake in San Francisco?

No — not without a professional inspection first. Even a moderate earthquake can crack the flue liner, dislodge the chimney crown, and shift masonry joints enough to create a carbon monoxide hazard or chimney fire risk, even if no damage is visible from inside the home. After any significant seismic event, schedule a Level 2 chimney inspection before using your fireplace. Learn more: Chimney Inspection
