Who We Are

Professional Chimney & Fireplace Experts in San Francisco

AL Chimney Service San Francisco is a professional chimney and fireplace service company dedicated to the safety and comfort of San Francisco homeowners and property managers. We specialize in the full range of chimney services including sweeping, inspection, repair, relining, waterproofing, and fireplace installation — serving all neighborhoods of San Francisco and the greater Bay Area.

Our team consists of certified chimney sweeps and fireplace technicians with deep knowledge of San Francisco's unique housing stock. From Victorian-era masonry chimneys in Pacific Heights to modern gas fireplace inserts in SoMa condominiums, we have the expertise to handle every type of chimney and fireplace system safely and correctly.

Why San Francisco Homeowners Trust Us

Certified Technicians

Our chimney sweeps are certified by the Chimney Safety Institute of America (CSIA) and follow NFPA 211 standards for all inspections and cleanings.

Available 24/7, 365 Days

Chimney emergencies don't follow business hours. We are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays, for emergency service throughout San Francisco.

Local San Francisco Expertise

We understand the specific challenges of San Francisco's coastal climate, seismic environment, and historic architecture. Our repairs are designed for Bay Area conditions.

20+ Services

From basic chimney sweeping to complete chimney rebuilding and fireplace installation, we handle every aspect of chimney and fireplace maintenance, repair, and improvement.

No Mess Guarantee

We use HEPA-filtered vacuum systems and protective drop cloths on every job. Your home stays clean during and after our service.

Transparent Pricing

We provide upfront, written estimates before starting any work. No surprise charges. No hidden fees.
