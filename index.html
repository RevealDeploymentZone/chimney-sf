What We Do

Complete Chimney & Fireplace Services in San Francisco

AL Chimney Service San Francisco offers a full range of chimney and fireplace solutions for residential and commercial properties throughout San Francisco, the Peninsula, and the East Bay. Every service is performed by certified technicians using professional-grade equipment.

Chimney Sweeping

Professional chimney sweeping removes creosote, soot, ash, and debris from your flue system. Creosote, a byproduct of burning wood, is highly flammable and its accumulation is the leading cause of chimney fires in San Francisco homes. Our certified sweeps use industry-standard rotary brushes, vacuum systems, and inspection cameras to clean your chimney safely and completely without creating a mess in your home. Annual chimney sweeping is recommended by the NFPA and required by many California homeowner insurance policies.

Learn More About Chimney Sweeping

Chimney Inspection

A thorough chimney inspection is the foundation of safe fireplace operation. AL Chimney Service San Francisco performs Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 inspections in accordance with NFPA 211 standards. Our Level 1 inspections cover accessible portions of the chimney structure. Level 2 inspections, required for home sales and after chimney events, include video scanning of the flue interior. Level 3 inspections address serious structural concerns. We provide detailed written reports and photographic documentation of all findings.

Learn More About Inspections

Chimney Cleaning

Deep chimney cleaning goes beyond basic sweeping to address heavy creosote deposits, glazed creosote (third-degree buildup), bird nests, animal debris, and other obstructions that can restrict airflow and create fire hazards. Our San Francisco chimney cleaning service uses professional rotary cleaning systems and chemical treatments to restore full draft performance and fire safety to your chimney system. Clean chimneys also improve heating efficiency and reduce heating costs.

Learn More About Chimney Cleaning

Chimney Repair

San Francisco's seismic activity, persistent fog, and salt air accelerate wear on chimney masonry and metal components. AL Chimney Service provides comprehensive chimney repair services including mortar joint repointing, flue tile replacement, damper repair, smoke chamber parging, firebox rebuilding, and exterior brick repairs. We diagnose the root cause of damage, not just the visible symptoms, ensuring lasting repairs that protect your home and investment. Our team responds to urgent repair calls 24 hours a day.

Learn More About Chimney Repair

Chimney Relining

A damaged or deteriorated chimney liner is a serious safety hazard. Cracks in clay tile liners allow combustion gases, including carbon monoxide, to penetrate into the living spaces of your San Francisco home. AL Chimney Service installs stainless steel flex liners, rigid stainless liners, and cast-in-place liners depending on your chimney configuration and appliance type. New liner installation also allows for proper sizing to match your wood-burning, gas, or oil-burning appliance, maximizing efficiency and safety.

Learn More About Chimney Relining

Chimney Cap Installation & Repair

A properly fitted chimney cap is one of the most cost-effective ways to protect your chimney from water damage, animal entry, and downdraft issues. AL Chimney Service San Francisco installs stainless steel and copper chimney caps sized and secured to your specific flue opening. We also repair or replace damaged caps that are loose, rusted, missing, or improperly fitted. In San Francisco's windy conditions, a quality chimney cap also prevents wind-induced downdrafts that push smoke back into the home.

Learn More About Chimney Caps

Chimney Crown Repair

The chimney crown is the concrete or mortar slab that covers the top of the chimney, sealing it except for the flue opening. Cracked or deteriorated crowns are a primary entry point for water into the chimney system. Water infiltration leads to spalling bricks, damaged liners, and costly structural repairs. AL Chimney Service San Francisco applies professional crown repair coatings and rebuilds severely damaged crowns to create a watertight seal that withstands San Francisco's seasonal rain and temperature swings.

Learn More About Crown Repair

Chimney Leak Repair

Water leaking from a chimney can cause extensive damage to ceilings, walls, insulation, and the chimney structure itself. Common sources of chimney leaks in San Francisco homes include damaged flashing, a cracked crown, missing or deteriorated cap, porous masonry, and failed mortar joints. AL Chimney Service conducts a systematic leak diagnosis and addresses the root cause, whether it requires flashing repair, crown sealing, masonry waterproofing, or a combination of treatments. We stop the leak and prevent recurrence.

Learn More About Chimney Leak Repair

Chimney Waterproofing

San Francisco's rainy winters and coastal moisture make chimney waterproofing an essential preventive maintenance step. AL Chimney Service applies professional-grade vapor-permeable waterproofing sealants to brick and mortar chimney exteriors. Unlike paint, masonry waterproofers allow moisture that has already penetrated to escape while blocking new water from entering, preventing freeze-thaw spalling and efflorescence. Waterproofing extends the lifespan of your chimney and is far more economical than masonry repair or rebuilding.

Learn More About Chimney Waterproofing

Frequently Asked Questions About Chimney Service in San Francisco

How often should I have my chimney swept in San Francisco?

The National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) recommends that chimneys, fireplaces, and vents be inspected at least once a year and cleaned as needed. In San Francisco, where fog and humidity can accelerate moisture damage, annual chimney sweeping is especially important to prevent creosote buildup and structural deterioration. If you use your fireplace frequently during the winter, you may need service more than once per year.

What does a chimney inspection include?

A professional chimney inspection by AL Chimney Service San Francisco includes a visual examination of the firebox, smoke chamber, damper, flue lining, chimney cap, crown, and exterior masonry. We check for creosote buildup, cracks, blockages, water damage, and any structural issues that could pose a fire or carbon monoxide hazard. Level 2 inspections include video scanning of the interior flue and are required before the sale of a home.

How do I know if my chimney needs repair?

Signs that your chimney may need repair include: white staining (efflorescence) on the exterior brickwork, crumbling mortar joints, a damaged chimney crown, water leaks in the fireplace, a strong odor from the fireplace when not in use, visible cracks in the flue tiles, smoke backing up into the room, or excessive soot around the firebox opening. If you notice any of these signs, contact AL Chimney Service San Francisco immediately.

What is chimney relining and why is it important?

Chimney relining involves installing a new liner inside your chimney flue. Liners protect the surrounding masonry from heat and combustion gases, improve draft efficiency, and prevent carbon monoxide from seeping into your home. Relining is required when the existing liner is cracked, deteriorated, or missing, or when upgrading to a new heating appliance with a different flue size requirement. A properly lined chimney is essential for safe fireplace or stove operation.

Can you remove animals or birds nesting in my chimney?

Yes. AL Chimney Service San Francisco provides safe and humane animal and nest removal from chimneys. Common intruders in San Francisco chimneys include birds, raccoons, squirrels, and bats. After removal, we clean and sanitize the flue and install a properly fitted chimney cap to prevent future animal entry. Do not attempt to light a fire if you suspect an animal or nest is present in your chimney.

Do you offer dryer vent cleaning in San Francisco?

Yes. Our dryer vent cleaning service removes lint buildup from your dryer duct system. Clogged dryer vents are a leading cause of house fires in the United States. We recommend professional dryer vent cleaning at least once per year, or more frequently for households with heavy laundry loads. Signs of a clogged dryer vent include clothes taking more than one cycle to dry, excessive heat from the dryer, or a burning smell during operation.

Are wood-burning fireplaces legal in San Francisco?

Wood-burning fireplace use in San Francisco is regulated by the Bay Area Air Quality Management District (BAAQMD). Burning is prohibited on Spare the Air days, which are declared when air quality forecasts show elevated particulate levels. Some new residential developments are restricted from installing new wood-burning appliances. AL Chimney Service can advise on compliant appliance options and help ensure your existing system meets current air quality and building code requirements.
